Shipping & delivery

  • We deliver all over Pakistan through trusted courier partners.
  • Delivery time: 2–3 working days in major cities, 3–5 working days in other areas.
  • Delivery charge: per order. Free delivery on orders above .
  • Every order is confirmed on WhatsApp before dispatch. Please keep your phone on so the rider can reach you.
  • You will receive your tracking number on WhatsApp once the parcel is dispatched.

Exchange & return

  • You can request an exchange within days of delivery.
  • Fabric must be uncut, unwashed and in its original packing with tags.
  • If you receive a damaged or wrong item, send us a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours and we will replace it at no extra cost.
  • For change-of-mind exchanges, delivery charges are paid by the customer.
  • Sale items can be exchanged but not refunded.

Payment

  • Cash on Delivery: pay the rider when your parcel arrives.
  • Advance payment: pay by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer and get % off your order. Send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp.
  • Please check the parcel in front of the rider if possible.

Privacy

We only use your name, phone number and address to deliver your order and contact you about it. We never sell or share your details with anyone except our courier partner.
