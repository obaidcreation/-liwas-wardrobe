FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer cash on delivery?
Yes. Cash on delivery is available all over Pakistan. You can also pay in advance by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer and save 5%.
How long does delivery take?
2–3 working days in major cities and 3–5 working days elsewhere. We share the tracking number on WhatsApp once your parcel is dispatched.
How much is delivery?
Rs 250 per order. Delivery is free on orders above Rs 5,000.
Can I exchange a suit?
Yes, within 7 days of delivery if the fabric is uncut, unwashed and in its original packing. See our exchange policy for details.
Will the colour be the same as the picture?
We use real photos, but screens show colours slightly differently. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will send a video in natural light before you order.
How do I place an order?
Add suits to your bag, fill in your details and tap “Place order on WhatsApp”. Your order opens in WhatsApp; press send and we confirm it with you.
Can I order from outside Pakistan?
Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the international shipping options and charges.
